Continuity Does Not Mean the Absence of Transition: A Dual-Rater Video Study of Action Boundaries in Copying Shi and Zhi from the Cao Quan Stele

Yiran Cheng · Sole author

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2027). Accepted; presentation forthcoming. Pittsburgh, USA · 10–14 May 2027.

Drawing on 72 dual-view recordings from four experienced calligraphers, independent dual-rater coding and practitioner reflections, this study distinguishes visible actions, phase functions and whole-character rhythm to inform the documentation of embodied craft knowledge.

From Visual Resemblance to Performability: Practitioner Knowledge in the Design of Digital Haining Shadow Puppets

Yiran Cheng · First author

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2027). Accepted; presentation forthcoming. Pittsburgh, USA · 10–14 May 2027.

Combining practitioner studies (n=10), regional LoRA adaptation and a bimanual rod-control system, this work examines how generated shadow-puppet images become articulated, performable characters. Its feedback framework connects pose conditions, component relationships and preservation conditions.
