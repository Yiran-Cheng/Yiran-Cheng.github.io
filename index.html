Digital humanities · Culture · Computation

Yiran Cheng

Cultural knowledge,
computational practice.

My work bridges cultural and media research, digital humanities and creative computing. Drawing on hands-on engagement with Chinese calligraphy, guqin and shadow puppetry, I use data analysis and interactive design to document, interpret and reactivate cultural heritage.

I combine close reading and practice-based inquiry with computational analysis, generative AI and web development, turning cultural materials into structured evidence, digital performances and experiences people can explore.

Projects & Practice

One thread across these projects: bringing cultural knowledge into forms people can inspect, perform, listen to and experience.

Enter the theatre

Shadow puppetry · Generative AI · Interaction

Haining Shadow Theatre

An interactive web theatre connecting Haining shadow-puppet traditions with digital character design and two-rod performance. Visitors can select a character and bring it to life on a bilingual stage.

My practice: building the interactive website and exploring how generated images become articulated, performable characters.

Explore the prototype

Calligraphy · Embodied knowledge · Evidence

From Trace to Gesture

A digital evidence prototype for the Cao Quan Stele. Compare contemporary writing pathways for shi (事) and zhi (之), inspect timing and contact events, and examine what a finished character can reveal about movement.

My practice: connecting calligraphic knowledge, video annotation and an interactive interface that makes interpretation and uncertainty visible.

Open calligraphy websiteRelated CHI paper
Enter the reading room

Guqin · Notation · Digital interpretation

Between the Strings

A bilingual guqin reading room bringing historical jianzipu tablature into dialogue with gesture, rhythm and sound. Readers can explore score sources and technique explanations, then review and listen to two pilot excerpts.

My practice: translating cultural and musical knowledge into a browser experience with source references, reading choices and interactive listening.

Explore the wrecks

Digital history · Maritime heritage · Spatial data

Shipwrecks Explorer

An interactive explorer of ancient Mediterranean shipwrecks, connecting a map, searchable register and timeline. Shared filters let visitors trace patterns across date, location and cargo, then inspect individual wreck records and references.

An independent interface to published Oxford Roman Economy Project and Harvard MAPS data, with English and Chinese views.

Continuity Does Not Mean the Absence of Transition: A Dual-Rater Video Study of Action Boundaries in Copying Shi and Zhi from the Cao Quan Stele

Yiran Cheng · Sole author

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2027). Accepted; presentation forthcoming. Pittsburgh, USA · 10–14 May 2027.

Drawing on 72 dual-view recordings from four experienced calligraphers, independent dual-rater coding and practitioner reflections, this study distinguishes visible actions, phase functions and whole-character rhythm to inform the documentation of embodied craft knowledge.

From Visual Resemblance to Performability: Practitioner Knowledge in the Design of Digital Haining Shadow Puppets

Yiran Cheng · First author

ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2027). Accepted; presentation forthcoming. Pittsburgh, USA · 10–14 May 2027.

Combining practitioner studies (n=10), regional LoRA adaptation and a bimanual rod-control system, this work examines how generated shadow-puppet images become articulated, performable characters. Its feedback framework connects pose conditions, component relationships and preservation conditions.
