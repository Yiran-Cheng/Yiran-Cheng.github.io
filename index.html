Academic & creative portfolio

Yiran Cheng

Media, culture & technology.

I am an MSc student in Media and Communications (Data and Society) at the London School of Economics and Political Science. My research explores media, cultural expression and participation.

This portfolio brings together my research and the things I make: interactive websites, digital heritage projects and documentary work. Each project connects a research question with a creative or technical process.
