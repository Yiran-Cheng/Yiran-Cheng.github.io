Haining Shadow Theatre

An interactive web theatre exploring how a traditional shadow puppet can become a digital character that an audience can perform.

Open the interactive theatre

Screenshot from the live theatre. The interface supports English and Chinese.

The question

From image to performance

What does it take for a digital shadow puppet to become performable? A recognisable appearance is one part of the problem; articulation, movement and the relationship between character and control also shape the experience.

The work

A theatre in the browser

I built an interactive website with selectable shadow-puppet characters, an online stage and two-rod controls. The project connects my work on generative imagery and style preservation with an audience-facing performance environment.

Visitors can explore the free-puppetry stage using mouse controls and browse the theatre’s bilingual content.
